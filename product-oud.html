OUD AL-BALGHITI

Inspiration: Noir 29 / Batch: 001

450 DH

Oud Al-Balghiti is a dark, nocturnal study of Moroccan leather and precious oud. Inspired by the dense, smoke-filled air of ancient medinas, it weaves together the raw depth of cedarwood, vetiver, and black tea, balanced by the fleeting sweetness of ripe fig. It is a fragrance that exists in the shadows, lingering close to the skin, whispering secrets of wood and spice.

Top Notes Bergamot, Black Tea, Fig
Heart Notes Cedar, Vetiver, Musk
Base Notes Amber, Oud, Leather

COMPOSITION & DOSAGE

FIG & BLACK TEA

20% Dosage

Provides a sweet, green, and slightly earthy brightness to balance the heavy resins.

MOROCCAN LEATHER

35% Dosage

Adds a warm, smoky, animalic depth that captures the spirit of Moroccan heritage.

AGARWOOD (OUD)

45% Dosage

The dark, resinous foundation. Woody, mysterious, and incredibly long-lasting.
